Privacy Policy for Room Rush

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Room Rush ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and discloses information when you download, install, and play our mobile game application.

1. Information We Collect

We respect your privacy and are committed to minimizing data collection. Room Rush is designed as a casual mobile game:

  • Gameplay & Telemetry Data: We may collect non-personally identifiable diagnostic data, such as game progress, session duration, level completion, and error logs, to balance levels and improve the game experience.
  • Device & Technical Information: We may collect non-identifying technical data such as device model, operating system version, screen resolution, and language settings to ensure optimal performance.
  • Personal Information: We do not collect personal identifying information such as your real name, physical address, phone number, or contacts.

2. How We Use Information

Information collected is used solely to:

  • Operate, optimize, and maintain the game.
  • Understand player progression and identify bugs or performance issues.
  • Enhance the user experience across different Android devices.

3. Third-Party Services & Analytics

The game may integrate trusted third-party SDKs for diagnostics, analytics, or services (such as Unity Services or Google Play Games Services). These third parties have their own privacy policies governing data handling in compliance with applicable laws.

4. Children's Privacy

Room Rush does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately so we can remove it.

5. Data Security

We implement commercially acceptable safeguards to protect any non-personal data collected against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

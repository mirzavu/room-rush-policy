Privacy Policy for Room Rush

Last updated: November 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Room Rush ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and discloses information when you download, install, and play our mobile game application.

1. Information We Collect

We respect your privacy and are committed to minimizing data collection. Room Rush is designed as a casual mobile game:

  • Gameplay & Telemetry Data: We collect gameplay progression, session duration, app interactions, crash logs, and diagnostics to balance levels, fix bugs, and improve the game experience.
  • Device & Advertising Identifiers: We and our analytics/attribution partners collect technical device information (such as device model, OS version, approximate country/region derived from IP address, App Set ID, and Google Advertising ID) for analytics, install attribution, and advertising measurement.
  • Personal Information: We do not collect personal identifying information such as your real name, physical address, phone number, or contacts.

2. How We Use Information

Information collected is used solely to:

  • Operate, optimize, and maintain the game.
  • Understand player progression, retention, and identify bugs or crashes.
  • Measure app install attribution and marketing campaign performance.

3. Third-Party Services & Analytics

Room Rush integrates trusted third-party SDKs for analytics, crash reporting, and install attribution, which may collect device identifiers (including Google Advertising ID) and app interaction data under their own privacy policies:

  • GameAnalytics: Gameplay telemetry, progression funnels, and crash diagnostics (GameAnalytics Privacy Policy).
  • Tenjin: Install attribution, retention analytics, and advertising campaign measurement (Tenjin Privacy Policy).
  • Google Play Services: App distribution, install referrer, and Advertising ID (Google Privacy Policy).

4. Children's Privacy

Room Rush does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately so we can remove it.

5. Data Security

We implement commercially acceptable safeguards to protect any non-personal data collected against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
